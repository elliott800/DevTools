The approach

Three principles behind everything we make.

01

Clarity first

We remove before we add. Every element earns its place or it leaves. The result feels obvious — which is the hardest thing to design.

02

Built to last

Boring technology, chosen on purpose. Fewer dependencies, cleaner repos, and code the next person can actually read a year from now.

03

In the details

Type that breathes, motion with restraint, spacing measured to the pixel. The craft lives in the parts you don't consciously notice.
